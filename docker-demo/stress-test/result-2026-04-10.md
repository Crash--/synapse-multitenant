## Findings

- **Tenant isolation held at 100 % in every run**, including the PgBouncer
  meltdown (run 6: 78 % request failures, isolation still 392/392).
- **PgBouncer session mode vs direct Postgres was a wash at 200 VUs** — the
  connection pool isn't the bottleneck.
- **The real bottleneck is the single-process event loop.** Extra connections
  don't help because Synapse serializes work through one asyncio loop.
  Scaling past 200 VUs will need tenant-aware workers.
